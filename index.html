I miss the way you looked at me

Like someone longs for Christmas on a summer break
I miss your lips against my skin,
Your touch, the way we used to lie
Skin to skin, and heart to heart,
Talking till the night would fall apart
Of all we’d been and all we’d be,
Just two scared kids, just you and me.

I miss your stupid goofy dance
If I could only get a glance
That would be enough for me
Just to refresh my memory

I miss crying in your arms
I miss your warming, loving hugs
I miss our long, unnecessary showers
That would last for hours and hours
I miss the kisses on my neck
And the bruises on my lip
I miss sleeping back to back
To protect against all bad.

It’s funny how, back then,
I always ended up on one inch of the bed.
There never seemed to be enough space
For two to fit, for two to stay.
But now I toss, I turn instead
In my 80 centimeter bed,
And somehow, in the quiet of the night,
It stretches wide from left to right.

I still wake when half asleep
Reaching for you under the sheets,
Searching for your silly snores,
Only to fall asleep again
Alone.

And if home is where the heart is,
Mine’s still there with you
Save an inch of bed for me,
I’ll squeeze right next to you.
